Part 18 · Start here

Art, music & creativity

Art and music are among the oldest pleasures humans have; creativity is a skill anyone can grow.

Appreciation is learnable: slowing down, knowing what to look and listen for, and understanding a little context transforms the experience. And creativity turns out to be less about lightning bolts and more about habits, quantity, constraints and play. This part covers both, with the myths (the Mozart effect, right-brained creatives, the lone genius) flagged.
