A calm, focused day

The practices with the best evidence, in the order of a day.

🌅 Start without the phoneLight, water, movement, one intention. Check messages after your first focus block.
🎯 One task per block25–50 minutes, notifications off, phone out of sight. Park distractions on paper.
🫁 Down-shift on cueTwo minutes of slow breathing with a longer exhale before a meeting or after a stressor.
🔁 Tie habits to cues"After I…, I will…". Make the good path easy and the bad path awkward.
🌳 Get outsideAim for around two hours a week in nature, in whatever chunks work.
🪷 A few minutes of stillnessBreath, body scan or kindness practice. The noticing is the practice.
🧭 Act on a valueOne small action for something that matters, especially on hard days.
🌙 Close the daySavour one good thing, park tomorrow's worries on paper, protect sleep.
