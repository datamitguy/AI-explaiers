Life & medicine

Understanding the basics of biology and medicine makes you a sharper patient, a better judge of health news, and more curious about being alive.

⚕️ Educational, not medical advice. For decisions about your health or treatment, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

You don't need a science degree to understand how genes, cells and immune systems work, or how a drug goes from idea to prescription. This part explains the essentials in plain English, links them to the health guide in Part 7, and flags the hype (epigenetic trauma, longevity pills, alternative remedies) alongside the genuinely astonishing (CRISPR cures, vaccines, evolution in action).

Life & medicine checklist

Useful habits.

💉 Keep vaccinations up to dateEspecially with immunosuppressive treatment: ask which ones and when.
💊 Take medicines as directedAnd tell every prescriber and pharmacist everything you take.
🦠 Antibiotics only when neededThey do nothing for viruses.
🧪 Ask about testsWhat will the result change? What are the false-positive risks?
📚 Check the evidence levelTrials beat anecdotes; reviews beat single studies.
🚩 Report side effectsThrough your clinician or national scheme.
🧬 Beware gene hypeMost traits involve many genes and environment.
🏃 Ageing wellExercise, sleep and not smoking still have the best evidence.
