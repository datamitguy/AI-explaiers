Part I · 11 models

🏛️ Stoicism: the art of living

A 2,300-year-old philosophy built for hard times: focus on what you control, judge wisely, and act well. It's the ancestor of modern cognitive therapy and a favourite of founders, generals and athletes.

A toolkit for Monday morning

Which tool to reach for, and when.

🏛️ When something goes wrongSeparate what's up to you from what isn't, question your first judgement, and ask how the obstacle can become the way.
💰 When thinking about moneyCreate value, own assets, keep your savings rate high and costs low, diversify, avoid expensive debt, and define "enough".
🧭 When decidingOne-way or two-way door? Which Cynefin domain? Run a pre-mortem on big bets and a quick decision matrix on close calls. Judge decisions by process, not outcome.
🔧 When solving a problemAsk why five times, map it with an issue tree or fishbone, look below the waterline with the iceberg, and lead with the answer when you communicate.
📋 When running a projectFind the critical path and the bottleneck, limit work in progress, estimate in ranges, and don't add people to a late project.
🧩 When building a productKnow which features are must-haves vs. delighters, fix the leakiest funnel stage, make the desired behaviour easy, and find product–market fit before scaling.
🧠 AlwaysCheck for survivorship bias, regression to the mean and incentives, steelman the other side, and keep a margin of safety.
