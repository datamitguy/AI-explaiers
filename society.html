Thinking clearly about society

Habits for contested questions.

🔍 Separate facts from valuesHume's is–ought gap: evidence can tell you what a policy does, not whether you should want it. Most political fights mix the two.
🧭 Steelman every ideologyState liberalism, conservatism, socialism or anarchism the way its best defenders would, before judging it.
📊 Check the replication recordSome famous psychology findings (Stanford prison, the marshmallow test) look very different today. Prefer robust results: conditioning, the Big Five, CBT.
🔭 Use several lensesConflict, function and meaning; Keynesian and Austrian; realist and liberal. Each sees something the others miss.
🏛️ Watch the institutionsRules, incentives, checks and balances, and who sets the agenda often matter more than who is in charge.
⚖️ Ask who bears the costBehind the veil of ignorance, which society would you choose? Who wins and loses from each idea in practice?
🌍 Remember history's recordIdeologies are judged by what they did, including famines, wars and repression, as well as by what they promised.
🤝 Stay curious about peopleThe fundamental attribution error, weak ties and contact: understanding others usually starts with meeting them.
