Home: family & food

Two things that fill most days: the people you live with and the food you eat. Small, evidence-based habits make both easier.

Educational, not medical advice. For concerns about a child's development or health, or dietary needs with a medical condition, talk to a health visitor, GP, paediatrician or dietitian.

Parenting advice and food advice are both drowning in fads. This part sticks to what the evidence supports, stays practical and non-judgemental, and pays attention to real constraints: limited time, money and energy, including living with a chronic illness.
