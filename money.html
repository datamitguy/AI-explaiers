Part 11 · Start here

Money

Money is a tool for security, freedom and the things you care about. The fundamentals are simple; the hard part is behaviour.

💷 Educational, not financial advice. Rules, allowances and products differ by country and change often. Check official sources and consider a regulated adviser for personal decisions.

Most of personal finance comes down to a few rules applied consistently: spend less than you earn, keep a cash buffer, clear expensive debt, invest simply and cheaply for the long term, protect against disasters, and don't let fear or excitement drive decisions. This part explains each one, the evidence behind it, and where rules differ between countries (with UK, US and India examples). It never recommends specific products.

A money checklist

The essentials, in order.

🧯 Build a bufferOne month of essentials first, then 3–6 (more with a health condition or irregular income).
🔥 Kill expensive debtCredit cards and overdrafts before investing; avalanche saves most.
🎁 Take the free moneyEmployer pension matches and tax relief first.
🧺 Invest simplyLow-cost, diversified index funds; keep fees low.
⏳ Stay investedDon't time the market; rebalance once a year.
🛡️ Protect the downsideIncome protection, life cover if others depend on you, a will.
🚨 Spot scamsUrgency, secrecy and guaranteed returns are red flags.
😊 Spend on what mattersTime, experiences and other people buy more happiness than stuff.
