Part 10 · Start here

Learning & memory

Most of us were never taught how to learn. The research is clear, surprising and very practical: the techniques that feel best often work least.

Memory isn't a recording; it's rebuilt each time you use it, and using it is what strengthens it. That single idea explains why testing yourself beats rereading, why spacing beats cramming, why sleep matters, and why a memory palace works. This part covers how memory works, the study techniques with the best evidence, memory tricks from the champions, how skills and expertise are built, and practical help for everyday forgetfulness and brain fog.

The lab

🗂️ The Memory Lab

Spaced-repetition flashcards: recall first, then rate yourself, and the lab schedules each card just before you'd forget it.

1 · AddOne idea per card, as a question. Ideas from this book, work terms, names, languages.
2 · RecallAnswer from memory before revealing. The effort is the point.
3 · RateAgain, Hard, Good or Easy. Cards you know move to longer gaps.
4 · ReturnA few minutes a day. Due cards appear when they need you.
Your cards

Privacy. Cards stay in this browser; export to back up or move devices. The schedule is a simple Leitner-box system (gaps of 1, 3, 7, 16, 35 and 80 days), the same principle as apps like Anki.

How to learn anything

The evidence-based essentials on one page.

🎯 Attend to encodeYou can't remember what you never noticed. One thing at a time, phone away.
🧩 Build a framework firstSkim the big picture, then fill in details; prior knowledge makes new facts stick.
🔁 Test, don't rereadClose the book and recall. Flashcards, practice questions, blank-page brain dumps.
📅 Space it outReview soon, then at growing gaps. A little daily beats a weekly cram.
🔀 Mix it upInterleave topics and problem types once the basics are in.
🗣️ Explain itTeach it, or explain it simply. Where you get vague is where to study next.
😴 Sleep on itSleep consolidates. Study, sleep, then test yourself.
🤖 Think first, then ask AIUse AI to quiz and give feedback, not to do the thinking for you.
