Craft: writing, career & design

Three crafts that multiply everything else you know: saying it clearly, building a career deliberately, and designing for real people.

Great ideas die in bad writing, careers drift without deliberate choices, and products fail when nobody watched a real person use them. The good news: all three are learnable crafts with clear principles and plenty of evidence. Each card ends with something to try this week.
