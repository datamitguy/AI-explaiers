# Master Glossary

**How to use this.** Every term and acronym used in the vault, in alphabetical order, with a one-line plain-English meaning and a link to the note that explains it properly. Keep it open in a side pane while you read, or while you sit in meetings. When someone says a word you do not know, search this page first.

| Assignment | A legal transfer of rights, such as the right to receive payments under a contract, to the lender as security. | [[11 Collateral and Security]] |
