Leadership & management

Leadership is about direction and people; management is about making organisations work. Most jobs need both.

These are the most taught, and most sold, ideas in business, so they need the most careful evidence ratings. Some hold up well (goal-setting theory, structured hiring, Deming's system thinking, basic management practices). Some are popular but weakly supported (situational leadership, Good to Great). Each card tells you which is which, and 6 simulations let you test the ideas yourself.

A manager's checklist

The evidence-backed basics, on one page.

🎯 Few, clear, hard goalsSpecific, stretching goals with feedback beat "do your best". Three to five key results, measured as outcomes, not activities.
📐 Structure and careGive people clear direction and genuine consideration. Passive, hands-off leadership is the worst style of all.
🎚️ Adapt per person, per taskDirect the beginner, coach the learner, support the hesitant, delegate to the expert.
🧱 Design the team firstA real team, a compelling direction, the right people and size, and support. Design beats day-to-day heroics.
🔴 Blame the system before the personMost variation is common cause. Improve the process instead of ranking people on noise.
🧑‍💼 Hire with structureSame questions, clear criteria, independent scores, a real work sample.
🔁 Learn in double loopsRun blameless reviews that change the rules, not just the actions.
🪞 Window and mirrorCredit the team for success; own the failures. And remember how much was luck.
