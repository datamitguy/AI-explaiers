Part 16 · Start here

How technology works

You use it every day. Knowing how it works makes you safer, calmer and better at choosing tools.

Most of technology is layers of simple ideas stacked very high: numbers become text, packets become web pages, statistics become chatbots. This part peels back the layers without jargon, then turns to the handful of security habits that stop most real-world attacks on individuals.

A digital safety checklist

Do these once, benefit for years.

🔑 Password managerUnique passwords everywhere; passkeys where offered.
📱 MFA on key accountsEmail, bank, cloud storage, social media.
📧 Secure your emailIt's the key to resetting everything else.
🔄 Auto-updatePhone, computer, browser, router.
💾 3-2-1 backupsThree copies, two media, one off-site; test a restore.
🎣 Pause on urgencyVerify requests for money or codes through another channel.
🗝️ Family code wordFor voice-clone scams.
🧹 Prune permissionsLocation, microphone, contacts.
